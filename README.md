Understanding Artificial Intelligence

At its core, artificial intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence processes by machines, particularly computer systems. These processes include learning (the acquisition of information and rules for using it), reasoning (using rules to reach approximate or definite conclusions), and self-correction. In the context of a course, students explore various AI methodologies, including machine learning, natural language processing, and neural networks. Each module aims to equip learners with the essential skills to implement AI solutions effectively.
